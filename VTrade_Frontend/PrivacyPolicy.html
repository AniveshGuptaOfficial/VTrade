Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2025 · VTrade Commerce Pvt. Ltd.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to provide our services, including name, phone number, email, VIT student ID, hostel block and room number, availability schedules (for student workers), and GPS location during active deliveries.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfil your orders and pickup requests
  • Match student workers with delivery opportunities based on V-TOP slots
  • Verify worker identity and student status
  • Enable the double-OTP and geofence security system
  • Calculate bounty points and salary for workers
  • Send delivery notifications, OTP codes, and service updates
  • Maintain audit logs for dispute resolution

3. Data Sharing

We share only what is necessary: delivery address and OTP with the assigned worker, product data with partner stores, and payment information (masked) with our payment gateway. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. OTPs expire within 30 minutes. Delivery proof photos are stored for 30 days (90 days if a dispute is open). Salary records are stored permanently for legal compliance. GPS location is captured only during active delivery sessions.

5. Your Rights

  • Request access to or deletion of your personal data
  • Update your profile, address, and contact details anytime
  • Workers can update availability slots anytime via the dashboard
  • Raise a data concern via our Contact page

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as VTrade evolves. Significant changes will be notified via the platform and email. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

For privacy concerns, please contact us. · Effective: January 2025
