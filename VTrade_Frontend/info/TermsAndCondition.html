2. Service Description

VTrade is a university-focused e-commerce and delivery platform built exclusively for VIT University students. It provides three core services:

  • Normal Shopping — groceries, beauty, undergarments, and stationery sourced from partner stores, delivered to your hostel.
  • Second-hand Marketplace — student-to-student listings with condition tags, in-app chat, and negotiated pricing.
  • Pickup Service — hire a student worker or hired delivery staff to collect parcels from campus karts (Amazon, Flipkart, etc.) and deliver to your room.

3. User Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate delivery information including hostel block and room number.
  • Ensure you or an authorised alternate receiver (pre-registered) is available to collect your order.
  • Use the platform lawfully, ethically, and in accordance with VIT University rules.
  • Do not share your OTP with anyone other than the assigned delivery worker at the time of handover.
  • Do not attempt to defraud the platform through false disputes, identity misuse, or payment manipulation.
  • Rate your delivery within 48 hours of receipt. Orders with 2+ unrated deliveries will be blocked from placing new orders.

⚠️ Users who choose Safe Drop or Peer Witness delivery options waive the right to raise a non-receipt dispute for that order.

4. Worker Rules & Obligations

Student Workers:

  • Must register with a valid VIT student ID and await admin verification before receiving assignments.
  • Must import V-TOP timetable slots and keep availability updated — these are the slots on which assignments are based.
  • Must accept or reject assignments promptly — repeated rejections may affect leaderboard ranking and assignment priority.
  • Must verify Pickup OTP-1 at the kart/store before collecting any parcel.
  • Must submit mandatory proof photo (in-app camera only) and verify Delivery OTP-2 at the recipient's door before marking delivered.
  • Must be within 150 metres of the delivery address (geofence) to mark an order complete.
  • Must clock in from within the VIT campus boundary — remote clock-in is blocked.

Hired Delivery Staff:

  • Fixed shifts are assigned and cannot be changed without admin approval.
  • Missed shift hours result in proportional salary deductions plus a penalty fee.
  • Absent without approved leave: full shift deducted + Rs. 50 penalty.
  • Late arrival beyond 15 minutes: proportional deduction + Rs. 20 fee.
  • Approved leave (max 2 days/month) and documented medical emergencies: no deduction.

5. Delivery & Security System

  • Double OTP: OTP-1 is verified at the pickup point; OTP-2 is sent to the buyer and verified at delivery. After 5 failed OTP attempts, the assignment is locked and escalated to admin.
  • Geofence: Workers must be within 150m of the delivery address and within the VIT campus boundary to complete delivery.
  • Photo Proof: Workers must upload an in-app timestamped GPS-tagged photo before marking delivery complete. Gallery uploads are blocked.
  • Open-box verification: For pickup service orders, workers must verify the parcel condition at the kart in the presence of the kart operator where possible.
  • Safe Drop: Worker leaves parcel at pre-specified location (e.g., hostel reception) and submits a photo. Buyer waives non-receipt dispute rights.
  • Peer Witness: Pre-authorised alternate receiver (with registered phone number and student ID) receives on buyer's behalf. Buyer waives non-receipt dispute rights.

6. Payments, Escrow & Refunds

All online payments are captured and held in escrow (status: held) at time of order. Escrow is released to the seller or worker only after:

  • Delivery is confirmed via both OTPs and geofence verification, and
  • No dispute is raised within the 24-hour dispute window.

If a dispute is raised, the escrow is frozen until admin resolution. Escrow is mandatory for all marketplace orders regardless of amount. COD orders are exempt from escrow.

Refunds for cancelled or dispute-resolved orders will be processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

💡 Scheduled delivery cancellations after 2 reschedules trigger automatic refunds. Rescheduling is allowed up to 1 hour before the delivery window opens.

7. Second-hand Marketplace

  • Only verified VIT students may list items. Listings must include an accurate condition tag (Like New / Good / Fair / For Parts).
  • Condition tags cannot be changed after a buyer has made an enquiry.
  • In-app chat is the only permitted communication channel. Sharing personal contact information (phone numbers, emails) is automatically blocked.
  • Buyers may submit a price offer; sellers may Accept, Decline, or Counter. An accepted offer generates a special checkout link at the negotiated price.
  • Chat is disabled once the listing is marked as sold.
  • Admin may remove listings and apply penalties for misrepresentation of condition or fraudulent activity.

8. Pickup Service

  • Students must select the correct kart/pickup point from the admin-managed list. Adding new pickup points is not permitted by users.
  • Up to 5 proof images may be uploaded when creating a request. At least 1 image is required when open-box verification is selected.
  • Dynamic pricing is calculated using: base fee (Rs. 10) + distance rate (Rs. 3/100m) + weight surcharge if applicable, subject to a minimum of Rs. 15 and a maximum cap of Rs. 80.
  • Price is shown with full breakdown before publishing the request and is locked at time of publish.
  • If the recipient is absent at delivery, only three fallback options are valid: pre-authorised alternate receiver, pre-approved safe-drop location, or return-to-hold status pending rescheduling.

9. Dispute Resolution

  • Disputes must be raised within 24 hours of the order being marked Delivered. After this window, dispute submission is blocked.
  • Disputes are reviewed by VTrade admin within 48 hours of submission.
  • Admin decisions on escrow release (refund or seller credit) are final unless an appeal is submitted within 48 hours.
  • Both buyer and worker history, delivery proof photos, OTP logs, and geofence data are considered during dispute review.
  • False disputes — disputes raised in bad faith and resolved against the raiser — are flagged and contribute to the blacklist threshold.

10. Blacklist & Penalty System

VTrade maintains a flag system to protect the community from abuse:

  • 1st verified offence: Formal warning issued.
  • 2nd verified offence: Account temporarily restricted (7 days for false disputes; 3 days for no-shows).
  • 3rd verified offence: Account blacklisted. Blacklisted users cannot place orders, list items, or send messages.

For delivery staff, repeated verified misconduct may result in permanent removal and referral to appropriate authorities. Serious fraud, theft, tampering, or identity misuse may be escalated by VTrade management to campus disciplinary authorities and law enforcement, depending on the severity and available evidence.

⚠️ Blacklisted users may appeal via the Contact page. Appeals are reviewed within 48 hours by VTrade admin.

VTrade acts as a facilitating platform between buyers, sellers, and workers. Disputes arising from misrepresentation of second-hand item conditions are handled through our dispute resolution process with escrow protection.
