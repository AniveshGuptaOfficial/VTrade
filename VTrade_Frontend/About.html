Our Story

Founded in 2025 by VIT students who were tired of wasting precious study hours walking to distant stores and karts, VTrade was born out of a simple frustration.

We asked: why not connect students who need things with students who have free time? What started as a WhatsApp group for hostel deliveries grew into a full platform.

Today, VTrade covers normal shopping, a second-hand marketplace with in-app chat and verified condition tags, and a pickup service that fetches your Amazon and Flipkart parcels from campus karts.
